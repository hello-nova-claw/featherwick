Featherwick · v4

Featherwick UI system

White paper, ink, and small dabs of watercolour taken from the bird art. Colour only appears where it describes a bird or an egg: feathers, eggs, friendliness and age. Everything else is ink and dashed stitch lines. Names and speech are set in Libre Caslon, and everything you read or count is set in Figtree.

For the shipped game, the dabs should be painted by the bird artist and used as images. The ones here are procedural stand-ins.

Primary is a soot pill with white text. Secondary is a white pill with a hairline. Tertiary is a word with a brushstroke under it. Every hit area is at least 44pt, even when the visible wash is smaller. Pressed buttons deepen the wash, and disabled buttons lose it and say why.

Filters are plain words. The selected one turns ink, gets weight 500 and an underline stroke, so it is marked by three things besides colour.

Notices on places
3 eggs waiting
A bird needs a name
The neighbour has news

A dab of paint and a short sentence, with no pill around it. The sentence carries the meaning and the dab only draws the eye.

Friendliness & age
Friendly
Pullet

Friendliness is four dabs of paint and age is three dots that grow, from chick to hen. An outline means not yet, and the stage is always written out.
