# proto-hen

Prototype parts cut from Anna's Procreate pencil layers (13 Oct 2026). Canvas 2048 x 2048.
Frame: ground point (between the feet) at (1130, 1889); bird height 1673 px (comb top at y 216).
Rig units: x right of the ground point, y up, in bird heights.

Parts (transparent PNG, ink alpha from pencil darkness):
- body: body outline + both thigh bulges (moved off the leg layers). Stray lines erased.
- leg_back, leg_front: shank + foot only. Pivot at the top of the shank, y 0.18.
  leg_back hip (-0.11, 0.18), leg_front hip (0.03, 0.18).
- neck: the hackle oval. For this heavy build it rides on the body and does not swing.
- head, comb, beak, eye: dip together at the throat (0.29, 0.79). Head/neck cut at row 545; needs overlap when painted.
- tail: rides on the body.

Measured joints (rig units): shoulder (0.15, 0.60), eye (0.365, 0.855), beak tip (0.44, 0.905),
comb base (0.34, 0.92), tail root (-0.14, 0.70), body tilt pivot (-0.03, 0.18).

hinge-test.png: rest, walk (22 deg), peck (body 22, head 40), glance (head 18).
