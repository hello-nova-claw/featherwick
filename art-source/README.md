# Art source

Source art that is not in the app. Files here do not ship: only `App/Featherwick/Art/` goes into the
bundle.

- `kit-template/`: a page to paint a kit over, and a starter `kit.json`. Made by
  `python3 scripts/make_kit_template.py`. See its `README.md`.
- `kit-fits/`: the kit skeleton fitted to a keeper's own silhouette, with a cut guide. Made by
  `python3 scripts/fit_kit_template.py <folder>`. See its `README.md`.
- `proto-hen/`: the first prototype parts, cut from Procreate pencil layers (13 Oct 2026). One-piece
  parts with no bones. Kept for reference. See its `README.md`.
